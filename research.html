Areas of work

Research Interests

Research focuses on efficient, reliable and secure computing and communication frameworks for emerging applications, combining machine learning, deep learning, optimization and intelligent decision-making.

Cybersecurity & Information Security

Intrusion detection, security mechanisms and practical defenses for connected systems and critical infrastructure.

Computer & Wireless Sensor Networks

Secure communication, energy efficiency, reliability and performance in computer and wireless sensor networks.

IoT Security

Secure wireless and IoT networks for smart systems, healthcare and next-generation communication.

Cloud & Privacy-Preserving Computing

Secure cloud environments and privacy-preserving computing across connected applications.

Intelligent Network Management

Machine learning, deep learning and optimization techniques for network security, resource efficiency and performance.
