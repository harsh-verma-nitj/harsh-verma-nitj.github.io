About

Prof. Harsh K. Verma is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Dr B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology Jalandhar. His academic work is centered on information security, computer networks, wireless ad-hoc networks and allied areas. Public institutional records also document his contributions to academic administration, curriculum governance and institutional committees.
