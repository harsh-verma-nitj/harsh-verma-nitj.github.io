About

I am a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at NIT Jalandhar. My work brings together cybersecurity, computer networks, wireless sensor networks, IoT security, cloud computing and AI-enabled secure systems.

My research addresses security, privacy, energy efficiency, reliability and performance, with applications in smart systems, healthcare and critical infrastructure. Alongside teaching and doctoral mentorship, I have served as Dean (Academic), Head of the Computer Centre and Head of CSE.
